Ways to work with us

  • Single reel: one vertical reel from a short shoot, ideal for testing
  • Content pack: three to five reels from one shoot day
  • Content day: a full day filmed across your business, cut into 8-10 reels
  • Social retainer: a monthly shoot plus 4-6 reels, planned around your offers

Made for the platform, not squeezed onto it

Everything is shot vertical, cut to the first-three-seconds rule, captioned for sound-off viewing and exported in the ratios each platform actually wants. No landscape corporate video awkwardly cropped into a Reel.

Consistency without the admin

A retainer means one planned shoot a month and a content calendar, so you are not scrambling for something to post. It pairs naturally with a flagship brand film and works especially well for a gym or a restaurant that lives on visuals.
