What we film for gyms

Most gym shoots start with one busy session and end with a full content library. In a half or full day on your floor we capture the pieces that actually convert enquiries into members:

  • Class and session energy: real members, real intensity, cut to music
  • Coach and PT profiles that put a face to your brand
  • Member testimonials, the single most persuasive video a gym can own
  • Facility tours for your website, Google Business Profile and paid ads
  • Vertical social media reels for Instagram, TikTok and Facebook

Why gyms need video, not just photos

A membership is an emotional purchase. People join a gym they can picture themselves in, and photos alone rarely carry that. A well-cut reel shows atmosphere, pace and community in ten seconds, which is exactly what a prospective member is scrolling for. For bigger launches or rebrands, a flagship brand film anchors the whole campaign.

Reels, testimonials and launch films
